## The course material

The course material began as a manuscript composed by hand, without AI. It has
since been through extensive copy-editing for both style and content, by human
and agentic readers alike. The agents were
[OpenAI's Codex](https://openai.com/codex/) mainly with `gpt-6-astra`, and
[Anthropic's Claude](https://claude.ai/) mainly with Opus 5. Every agent edit
passed a human review for correctness and style before publication.

## The website

The site's code, its interactive apps and the tooling behind the slides rest on
human-written design specifications. Agents carried out the implementation.
Nothing reaches the site without a human review against the specification and
for the general soundness of the code.

Responsibility for the result, and for any errors in it, remains with the
author.

## Further reading

The [Leiden Declaration on Artificial Intelligence and
Mathematics](https://leidendeclaration.ai/) concerns the use of AI in
mathematical research rather than in teaching material, so it does not govern a
course like this one. Its recommendations nevertheless set the standard this
page follows: disclose the tools used, take responsibility for the correctness
of published work, and affirm human authorship.
