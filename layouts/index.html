Logical methods for AI

Logic is the study of correct reasoning, and reasoning is what artificial intelligence sets out to automate. This is an open introduction to the logical foundations of AI: how formal languages represent knowledge, how algorithms search for proofs, and where the limits are.

Learning goals

  • Identify where logic is applied within AI.
  • Explain the strengths and weaknesses of logic within AI.
  • Represent knowledge using logical formulas.
  • Apply algorithms for automated reasoning.
  • Evaluate inferences in AI contexts for validity.
