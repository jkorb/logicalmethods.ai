# Model worlds

`fol-worlds` composes the established world outline and object SVGs with live
notation to show three stages: unanalyzed scenarios, pictured SUN and RAIN facts, and
first-order structure. Arrows connect the stages. On narrow screens the stages
stack vertically. The diagram uses the app stylesheet loaded by the chapter's
`logic-app` shortcodes.

The drawing reuses the outline from `model-world.svg` and the pictured objects
from the shared drawing assets. Notation and labels remain text in the
shortcode, rather than rasterized mathematics. Colors follow the set-diagram
theme tokens.
